# In the waiting room

When the server restarts or a maintenance runs, you land in the **waiting room** - so you are not kicked.

## What you see
- **Above your hotbar** it says how long it takes, e.g. "Restart · about 0:40 left". The **clock in your hand** says what is going on ("⏳ Restart", "🛠 Maintenance until 18:00").
- **The bossbar** at the top names the reason and fills up until the expected return.
- **On joining** one line in the chat - hover over **[Info]** for details.

## Going back
You do not have to do anything: as soon as the server is ready, the waiting room sends you back by itself ("Going back to the server …"). Whoever was there first goes first.

Impatient? **`/main`** takes you back right away - but only if the server is already ready.

> [!NOTE]
> During a **maintenance** you stay in the waiting room until it is over. Then you go back by yourself as well.

## Good to know
- There is no damage and no hunger in the waiting room; your inventory from the main server stayed safe there.
- The chat is usually off in the waiting room.
- If you do not get back several times (e.g. because you are not on the whitelist of the main server), the waiting room disconnects you with a note.
